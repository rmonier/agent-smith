# Third-Party Notice

A third-party notice records attribution, authorship, and upstream source information for imported or bundled material. It helps preserve provenance tracking and supports licensing and attribution by making the origin of reused content explicit.

## Why it matters

- Preserves credit for the original author or organization.
- Makes downstream reuse and compliance review easier.
- Distinguishes source provenance from the functional content of the file.
- Supports open source attribution and broader license compliance requirements.

## In the source document

The notice in [[summaries/agents__skills__subagent-profile-adapter__NOTICE]] identifies:

- The component name: `subagent-profile-adapter`
- The hosting project: `agent-smith`
- The copyright holder: Romain Monier
- The original upstream repository: `https://github.com/rmonier/agent-smith`

This is a compact example of a third-party notice used to preserve source attribution while packaging a component into another repository.

## Related ideas

- upstream source attribution: records where material originally came from.
- source provenance: tracks the origin and lineage of imported content.
- attribution based reuse: reuse that depends on keeping attribution intact.
- third party notice: the notice artifact itself as a compliance record.
- knowledge linking and citations: connecting source documents to derived knowledge.
