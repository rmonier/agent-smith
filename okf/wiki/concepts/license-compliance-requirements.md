# License Compliance Requirements

License compliance requirements are the conditions a user must satisfy to lawfully use, modify, and redistribute licensed material. In the Apache 2.0 text from [[summaries/agents__skills__subagent-profile-adapter__LICENSES__Apache-2-0-txt]], these requirements define what must be preserved, disclosed, and included when work is shared onward.

## What compliance means

- Follow the license terms that apply to the work, including any redistribution conditions.
- Preserve required notices and attribution when distributing source or derivative works.
- Include the license text with redistributed copies of the work.
- Mark modified files so recipients can distinguish original material from changes.
- Respect any special notice-file obligations when the original work includes a `NOTICE` file.

## Core requirements in Apache 2.0

The Apache License 2.0 combines broad reuse rights with specific compliance duties:

- A copy of the license must accompany redistributed work or derivative works.
- Modified files must carry prominent notices that they were changed.
- Source distributions of derivative works must retain applicable copyright, patent, trademark, and attribution notices.
- If the original work includes a `NOTICE` file, derivative works must include its readable attribution notices where required.
- Additional attribution notices may be added, but they cannot alter the license itself.

## Why this matters

Compliance requirements are what make permissive licensing operational rather than merely permissive. They:

- Preserve provenance and attribution across reuse.
- Reduce ambiguity for downstream recipients.
- Support [[concepts/licensing-and-attribution]] and [[concepts/open-source-attribution]].
- Reinforce [[concepts/provenance-tracking]] and [[concepts/source-provenance]] in compiled knowledge and software distribution contexts.
