# License Conditions

[[concepts/license-conditions]] describes the obligations attached to using and sharing licensed material. In the context of [[summaries/agents__skills__subagent-profile-adapter__LICENSES__Apache-2-0-txt]], these conditions define what recipients must do when they redistribute the Apache License 2.0 work or any derivative version of it.

## What the concept covers

- The legal permissions granted by a license are paired with required terms for redistribution.
- Conditions can apply to source distributions, object distributions, and modified files.
- License terms may require notice preservation, attribution retention, and inclusion of the license text.
- Some licenses also specify how contributions are treated and whether additional terms are allowed.

## Apache 2.0 conditions in this source

The Apache License 2.0 source file emphasizes these conditions:

- Distribute a copy of the license with the work or derivative work.
- Mark modified files so recipients can tell they were changed.
- Keep copyright, patent, trademark, and attribution notices from the source form.
- Include relevant `NOTICE` text when the original work provides one.
- Preserve compliance even when adding your own copyright statement or extra terms for your changes.

## Why this matters

- It makes reuse predictable by pairing broad permissions with explicit obligations.
- It protects attribution and provenance across downstream distributions.
- It supports [[concepts/attribution-based-reuse]] by allowing reuse while requiring notice handling.
- It connects directly to [[concepts/license-compliance-requirements]] and [[concepts/licensing-and-attribution]].
