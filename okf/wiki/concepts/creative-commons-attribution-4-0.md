# Creative Commons Attribution 4.0

Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, often written as CC BY 4.0, is a [[concepts/permissive-open-source-licensing]] model that allows broad reuse of licensed material as long as users give appropriate credit. It is designed for sharing creative works, data, and other copyrightable material with minimal restrictions while preserving [[concepts/attribution-based-reuse]] obligations.

The full legal text appears in [[summaries/agents__skills__subagent-profile-adapter__LICENSES__CC-BY-4-0-txt]] and [[summaries/agents__skills__skill-creator__LICENSES__CC-BY-4-0-txt]].

## Core idea

CC BY 4.0 grants the public a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable license to copy, share, and adapt the licensed material. The license is intentionally flexible across media and formats, including technical changes needed to use the material.

Its main tradeoff is simple: reuse is broadly allowed, but attribution must be preserved when the material is shared.

## Key permissions

- Reproduce and share the licensed material in whole or in part.
- Create, reproduce, and share adapted material.
- Make technical modifications needed to use the material in different media or formats.
- Receive an automatic offer of the same licensed rights when the material is redistributed downstream.
- Rely on the license across jurisdictions where the rights apply.

## Key obligations

- Retain attribution details supplied by the licensor, including creator names, copyright notice, license notice, and warranty disclaimer notice.
- Include a URI or hyperlink to the licensed material when reasonably practical.
- Indicate when the material has been modified and preserve prior modification notices.
- Include a reference to the CC BY 4.0 license text or URI.
- Avoid imposing extra restrictions or effective technological measures that would block downstream exercise of the licensed rights.
- Ensure any adapter's license does not prevent downstream recipients from complying with CC BY 4.0.
- Remove attribution information if the licensor requests it, to the extent reasonably practicable.

These requirements make CC BY 4.0 a useful model for knowledge-linking and citations in reused content, because the license expects clear provenance and visible credit.

## Important boundaries

CC BY 4.0 does not license every right associated with a work. In particular:

- Moral rights, publicity rights, and privacy rights are generally outside the grant, though some waiver may apply where possible.
- Patent and trademark rights are not licensed.
- Copyright exceptions and limitations, such as fair use or fair dealing, remain available outside the license.
- The license does not imply endorsement or sponsorship by the licensor.
- The material is provided as-is, with warranties and liability broadly disclaimed to the extent allowed by law.

This boundary between granted rights and reserved rights is a useful example of [[concepts/license-conditions]] and [[concepts/license-compliance-requirements]].

## Database rights

The license also addresses sui generis database rights where they apply. In that case, it allows extraction, reuse, reproduction, and sharing of all or a substantial portion of a database, while preserving the attribution obligations that apply to shared database contents.

If a reused database becomes a new database in which the reuser holds sui generis database rights, that new database can count as adapted material under the license.

## Term and termination

The license lasts for the term of the licensed rights, but rights terminate automatically on violation. They may be reinstated if the violation is cured within 30 days of discovery or if the licensor expressly reinstates them.

The licensor can also offer the same material under separate terms or stop distributing it without ending the CC BY 4.0 grant.

## Why it matters

CC BY 4.0 is one of the most common open content licenses because it balances reuse freedom with creator recognition. It is especially important in workflows that depend on source provenance, provenance tracking, and reusable documentation ecosystems.

Within this wiki, it also connects to [[concepts/generated-content-governance]] and [[concepts/documentation-source-priority]], since licensed source material must be handled with clear attribution and careful boundary management.
