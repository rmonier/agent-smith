# Apache License 2.0

The Apache License 2.0 is a permissive open-source license that allows broad reuse, modification, sublicensing, and redistribution while requiring notice preservation, preserving an optional NOTICE file, and providing an explicit patent grant.

## What it allows

- Use the work for almost any purpose.
- Reproduce and distribute copies in source or object form.
- Prepare derivative works and add additional or different terms for modifications, as long as the original license conditions are still met.
- Receive a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free copyright license.
- Receive a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive patent license from each contributor for claims necessarily infringed by their contributions.

## What it requires

- Include a copy of the license when redistributing the work or derivative works.
- Mark modified files with prominent notices stating that changes were made.
- Retain applicable copyright, patent, trademark, and attribution notices from the source form.
- Preserve NOTICE file attribution text when the original work includes one, while allowing extra attribution notices that do not modify the license.
- Respect the rule that contributions are generally licensed under the same terms unless a separate agreement says otherwise.

## Key legal features

- The license defines clear boundaries between the original work, contributions, derivative works, and separate agreements.
- Trademark rights are not broadly granted beyond reasonable reference to the work and any NOTICE content.
- The patent grant terminates if a recipient initiates patent litigation alleging the work or an incorporated contribution infringes.
- The license disclaims warranties and limits contributor liability.
- A redistributor may offer support, warranty, indemnity, or other liability terms on their own behalf.

## Why it matters

This license is widely used because it combines permissive reuse with explicit patent terms and modest redistribution obligations. It is a common choice for projects that want broad adoption without copyleft requirements while keeping attribution and provenance visible.
