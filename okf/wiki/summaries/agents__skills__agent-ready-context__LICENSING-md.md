# Summary

This document spells out the licensing terms for the `agent-ready-context` skill. It separates the skill into Creative Commons and Apache-licensed parts, notes a copied fallback in `scripts/editorial_pass.py`, and points readers to the full license texts and third-party notices.

## Key Points

- `SKILL.md`, original content in `references/` and `assets/`, and other original documentation are licensed under [[concepts/creative-commons-attribution-4-0]] (`CC-BY-4.0`).
- Original executable files under `scripts/` are licensed under the [[concepts/apache-license-2-0]] (`Apache-2.0`).
- `scripts/editorial_pass.py` includes a small OpenKB-derived fallback, which is also treated as Apache-2.0-compatible code.
- The copied symbols, upstream source, and copyright notice for that fallback are documented in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- The skill attributes copyright to Romain Monier and identifies `agent-smith` as the original source project.
- Full license texts are stored under `LICENSES/`.
