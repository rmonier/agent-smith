## Overview
This file contains the full text of the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Public License (CC BY 4.0), along with Creative Commons' introductory notes and legal disclaimers. It defines the standard terms for sharing, modifying, and attributing licensed material.

## Key Points
- Grants a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable license to reproduce, share, and adapt the licensed material.
- Requires [[concepts/attribution-based-reuse]] when the material is shared, including creator credit, copyright notice, license notice, warranty disclaimer notice, and a link or URI to the material when practical.
- Requires indication of modifications and preservation of prior modification notices.
- Prohibits adding legal or technical restrictions that would limit recipients' ability to exercise the licensed rights.
- Explicitly states that patent, trademark, moral, privacy, and publicity rights are not licensed except for limited waivers where needed to exercise the license.
- Includes a strong warranty disclaimer and liability limitation.
- Provides automatic termination for noncompliance, with possible reinstatement if the violation is cured within 30 days or the licensor reinstates rights.

## Concepts
- [[concepts/licensing-and-attribution]]: A standard public license that defines reuse rights and obligations.
- [[concepts/attribution-based-reuse]]: The obligation to credit creators and retain required notices.
- [[concepts/license-conditions]]: Modified works derived from licensed content.
- [[concepts/permissive-open-source-licensing]]: Creative Commons notes that the license text itself is dedicated under CC0.

## Practical Implications
- The license is designed to make reuse simple and predictable across media and formats.
- Downstream users automatically receive the same offer to exercise the licensed rights.
- If you distribute adapted material, your chosen terms for that adaptation cannot block compliance with CC BY 4.0.
- The licensor can still distribute the same material under other terms separately, without ending this license.

## Notes
- Creative Commons is not a law firm and provides the text on an as-is basis.
- The document emphasizes that the public license covers only rights the licensor can actually grant under copyright and similar rights.
- The Creative Commons trademark notice is included separately and is not part of the license terms.
