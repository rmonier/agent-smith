# Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License

This document is the full legal text of the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Public License, usually called CC BY 4.0.

## What it grants

- A worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable license to reproduce and share the licensed material.
- Permission to create, reproduce, and share adapted material based on the original work.
- Coverage across media and formats, including technical modifications needed to use the work.

## Core conditions

- [[concepts/attribution-based-reuse]] is required when sharing the material, including modified versions.
- The licensor's requested attribution details must be retained when supplied, such as creator names, copyright notice, license reference, warranty disclaimer reference, and a link or URI to the work where practical.
- Modifications must be indicated, and prior modifications must be preserved where applicable.
- Any adapter's license applied to derivative work must not block recipients from complying with CC BY 4.0.

## Important limits and exclusions

- The license does not cover moral rights, publicity rights, privacy rights, patents, or trademarks.
- Exceptions and limitations to copyright, such as fair use or fair dealing, are outside the license and do not require compliance with it.
- No endorsement is implied by use of the licensed material.

## Database rights

- The license also addresses database rights when the work includes sui generis database rights.
- It allows extraction, reuse, reproduction, and sharing of substantial database contents.
- Sharing a substantial portion of a licensed database requires attribution compliance.

## Warranty and liability

- The material is provided as-is and as-available.
- The licensor disclaims warranties to the fullest extent allowed by law.
- The licensor limits liability for damages arising from use of the material.

## Term and termination

- The license lasts for the duration of the covered rights.
- Rights terminate automatically on breach, with possible reinstatement if the violation is cured within 30 days of discovery or if the licensor expressly reinstates them.
- The licensor may also offer the material under separate terms or stop distributing the material without terminating this license.

## Interpretation notes

- The license should not be read to restrict lawful uses that do not need permission.
- Unenforceable provisions are to be reformed or severed as needed to preserve the rest of the license.
- No waiver occurs unless the licensor expressly agrees.

## Significance

- CC BY 4.0 is a standard [[concepts/permissive-open-source-licensing]] framework for broad reuse with attribution.
- It is designed to be flexible across jurisdictions and formats while preserving author credit.
- The license text itself notes that Creative Commons is not a party to the license and that the text is dedicated to the public domain under CC0.
