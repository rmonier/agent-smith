# Apache License 2.0

This document contains the full text of the Apache License, Version 2.0, a permissive open-source license that defines how a work may be used, modified, and redistributed.

## Key points

- Grants broad rights to use, reproduce, modify, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute the licensed work.
- Includes a patent license from each contributor for claims necessarily infringed by their contributions.
- Requires redistribution of the license text and preservation of certain notices in derivative works.
- Allows modified works to carry additional or different license terms, as long as the original license conditions are still met.
- Includes standard provisions for trademarks, warranty disclaimer, limitation of liability, and optional warranty or indemnity terms.

## Core concepts

- [[concepts/permissive-open-source-licensing]]: The license is a canonical example of a permissive open-source license.
- [[concepts/license-compliance-requirements]]: Defines the copyright permissions granted to recipients.
- patent license: Separately grants patent rights tied to contributor submissions.
- redistribution requirements: Specifies what must accompany copied or modified versions.
- disclaimer of warranty: States the work is provided "AS IS" without warranties.
- limitation of liability: Limits contributor liability for damages arising from use of the work.

## Notable terms

- **Licensor**: The copyright owner or authorized entity granting the license.
- **Contributor**: A party whose contribution has been incorporated into the work.
- **Derivative Works**: Modified or derived works that remain within the license's scope.
- **NOTICE file**: A distribution artifact whose attribution notices must be preserved when present.

## Practical meaning

The license is designed to make reuse straightforward while preserving attribution and legal clarity. It is especially common in software projects that want wide adoption with minimal copyleft obligations.
