# CC BY 4.0 License

This document is the full text of the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Public License ([[concepts/creative-commons-attribution-4-0]]). It defines the legal terms for sharing, adapting, and redistributing licensed material as long as attribution and other stated conditions are followed.

## Core purpose

CC BY 4.0 grants broad reuse rights for creative and other copyrighted material while preserving author attribution. It is designed to be legally reusable across media, formats, and jurisdictions, and it explicitly allows both direct reuse and creation of adapted material.

## Main permissions

- Worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable license to use the licensed material.
- Permission to reproduce and share the original material.
- Permission to create, reproduce, and share adapted material.
- Permission to make technical modifications needed to use the material in different media or formats.
- Automatic downstream offer of the same licensed rights to recipients.

## Main obligations

- Keep attribution notices when sharing the material, including creator identification, copyright notice, license notice, warranty disclaimer notice, and a link or URI when practical.
- Indicate if the material was modified and retain prior modification notices.
- Include a reference to the CC BY 4.0 license text or URI.
- Do not impose additional restrictions or effective technological measures that limit others from exercising the licensed rights.
- If the licensor requests removal of attribution information, remove it where reasonably practicable.

## Important limits

- The license does not grant patent or trademark rights.
- Moral rights, privacy rights, publicity rights, and similar rights are not fully licensed, though some waiver may apply where possible.
- The material is provided as-is, with no warranties.
- Liability is broadly disclaimed to the extent allowed by law.
- The license terminates automatically on violation, with possible reinstatement if cured within 30 days or expressly reinstated.

## Database rights

The license also addresses database rights and permits extraction, reuse, reproduction, and sharing of substantial database contents when those rights apply. If a database is reused into a new database where the user has sui generis rights, that new database may count as adapted material.

## Notable interpretation points

- Exceptions and limitations such as fair use are outside the license.
- The license cannot reduce rights that already exist under law.
- Invalid provisions are reformed or severed to preserve the rest of the license.
- Creative Commons itself is not a party to the public license text, and its trademarks remain protected.

## Why it matters

This license is one of the most widely used open licensing standards for content sharing. It enables broad reuse while keeping the attribution chain intact, making it a foundational example of licensing and attribution, open-source attribution, and permissive open-source licensing.
