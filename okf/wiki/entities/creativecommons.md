# Creative Commons

Creative Commons is the nonprofit organization behind the CC licensing suite referenced in this repository's licensing documents.

## Key Facts

- The CC BY 4.0 text in [[summaries/agents__skills__subagent-profile-adapter__LICENSES__CC-BY-4-0-txt]] identifies Creative Commons as the publisher of the license and states that its public licenses are available on an as-is basis.
- The license text emphasizes that Creative Commons is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or legal services.
- Creative Commons dedicates the text of its public licenses to the public domain under CC0, while keeping the Creative Commons name and logo protected as trademarks.
- The license file uses the formal license name `Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Public License` and notes that it is part of the Creative Commons public license family.
- Creative Commons is named in the attribution and license text for the skill package materials, including the CC BY 4.0 licensing path used for documentation assets.
- The licensing split in [[summaries/agents__skills__skill-creator__LICENSING-md]] uses Creative Commons licensing for original documentation and Apache 2.0 for executable scripts.
