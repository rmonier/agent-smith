# Licenses Directory

The `LICENSES/` directory is the location where the complete license texts for the `subagent-profile-adapter` skill are stored.

## Key Facts

- It contains the full texts for the licenses referenced by the skill's licensing notice.
- The skill points to `LICENSES/` as the authoritative place for the `CC-BY-4.0` and `Apache-2.0` license texts.
- Its presence supports [[concepts/license-segmentation-by-file-type]] and [[concepts/license-compliance-requirements]].
- It is part of the licensing structure described in [[summaries/agents__skills__subagent-profile-adapter__LICENSING-md]].
- It is related to the broader licensing and attribution framework used by [[entities/subagent-profile-adapter]] and [[entities/creative-commons-attribution-4-0-international-public-license]].

## Role In The Skill

This directory helps make license terms easy to inspect and keeps the skill's legal notices organized alongside the files they govern. That arrangement reinforces [[concepts/licensing-and-attribution]] and [[concepts/open-source-attribution]].
