# Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Public License

The Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Public License is the standard Creative Commons license text used in this repository's original documentation and related non-code materials. It defines a broad reuse grant with attribution requirements, downstream sharing conditions, and clear limits on warranties and liability.

## Role In This Repository

- Covers original documentation such as `SKILL.md`, `LICENSING.md`, and other authored materials distributed with skill packages.
- Appears alongside [[entities/apache-license-2-0|Apache License 2.0]] in mixed-license distributions where code and documentation are separated.
- Supports [[concepts/attribution-based-reuse]] while allowing copying, redistribution, and adaptation of licensed material.
- Establishes that the license text itself is dedicated to the public domain under CC0, separate from the licensed material it governs.
- Serves as the legal basis for sharing adapted material as long as attribution and license-retention conditions are preserved.

## Provenance

- The license text comes from Creative Commons Corporation and is presented with the standard Creative Commons disclaimer that it is not legal advice.
- The license grants a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable license for the Licensed Material, subject to compliance with its terms.
- It allows reproduction, sharing, and creation of adapted material, including technical modifications needed to use the material in different media and formats.
- It requires attribution, notice retention, modification marking, and a link or URI to the license when material is shared.
- It also prohibits adding downstream restrictions or effective technological measures that would limit other recipients' rights.
- The license disclaims warranties, limits liability, and states that patent, trademark, and most moral/privacy/publicity rights are not licensed.
- Rights terminate automatically on noncompliance, with possible reinstatement after cure within 30 days of discovery or express reinstatement by the licensor.
- The text also addresses sui generis database rights, making clear that substantial reuse of database contents is covered where those rights apply.
- Complete license texts are stored under `LICENSES/`.
