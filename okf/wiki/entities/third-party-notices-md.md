# Third Party Notices

`third-party-notices.md` is the companion notice file for the `skill-creator` materials. In the context of `entities/agents-skills` and the broader `skill-creator` package, it is identified as the authoritative location for the exact passages and sources adapted from Anthropic's and OpenAI's skill-creator skills.

## What it records

- The specific reused or adapted passages included in the `skill-creator` directory.
- The original source attribution for those passages, including upstream repository references.
- The licensing basis for the reused material, which is noted as Apache-2.0.
- A provenance note that the upstream copyright holders, years, and licence terms were retrieved directly from the cited upstream files and should be re-verified before any new adaptation.
- A documented comparison result showing that most of the file is independent expression, with only three short passages closely tracking upstream wording.
- Its place inside a repository that also contains other license, notice, and attribution files such as `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, `LICENSING.md`, and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, alongside the `.agents/skills/` tree.

## Why it matters

This file supports [[concepts/attribution-based-reuse]] and [[concepts/license-compliance-requirements]] by making reuse traceable and reviewable. It also fits the broader practice of [[concepts/open-source-attribution]] and [[concepts/licensing-and-attribution]] in shipped skill content.

It also reinforces [[concepts/source-provenance]] and [[concepts/provenance-tracking]] by separating independent expression from passages that require notice under Apache-2.0. The document explicitly calls out that upstream state can change, so reuse decisions should be checked against current sources.

Within the repository snapshot, this notice also sits among a larger set of governance and packaging files, which makes it part of the repository's [[concepts/repository-structure-overview]] and [[concepts/repository-inventory]] rather than a standalone artifact.
