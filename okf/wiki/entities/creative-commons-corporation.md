# Creative Commons Corporation

Creative Commons Corporation is the organization behind the Creative Commons licensing suite, including CC BY 4.0, used for sharing copyrighted material under standardized attribution-based terms.

## Key Facts

- The document identifies Creative Commons Corporation as the source of the `CC-BY-4.0` license for repository material.
- It is associated with the full license name [[entities/creative-commons-attribution-4-0-international-public-license]] and the shorthand [[concepts/creative-commons-attribution-4-0]].
- The license grants broad reuse rights, including redistribution and adaptation, while requiring attribution and compliance with stated conditions.
- The license text emphasizes that Creative Commons is not a law firm and provides the license on an as-is basis with warranty and liability disclaimers.
- The license text also notes that Creative Commons is not a party to the public license, while its trademarks remain protected.
- The licensing split in [[summaries/agents__skills__skill-creator__LICENSING-md]] treats Creative Commons licensing as the governing terms for original docs, references, and assets.
- The document places Creative Commons licensing within a broader licensing and attribution structure that also includes Apache 2.0 for scripts.
