# Creative Commons

Creative Commons is a nonprofit organization that publishes standardized public copyright licenses for sharing creative and other works.

## Role

- Provides the Creative Commons public license suite, including [[concepts/creative-commons-attribution-4-0]]
- Offers licenses that let creators grant reuse permissions under clear, standardized terms
- Publishes guidance for licensors and licensees on how to apply and use its licenses
- Appears in the `agent-ready-context` skill licensing notice as the publisher of the CC BY 4.0 terms applied to `SKILL.md`, `references/`, `assets/`, and other original documentation
- Issues the legal text for CC BY 4.0 as a standardized public license with broad reuse and attribution requirements
- Is referenced in the `subagent-profile-adapter` licensing file as the source of the CC BY 4.0 text applied to repository documentation

## In the CC BY 4.0 license text

- The organization states that it is not a law firm and does not provide legal services or legal advice
- Its licenses are made available on an "as-is" basis, with no warranties
- The text emphasizes that licensors should read and understand the terms before applying them
- The license allows broad reuse, including adaptation, subject to attribution and other conditions
- The public license grants worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable rights to reproduce, share, and adapt licensed material
- The license text says Creative Commons is not a party to its public licenses, even though it may sometimes apply them to material it publishes

## Key legal framing

- Creative Commons licenses are irrevocable once applied, subject to the license terms
- The licenses grant permissions under copyright and certain other related rights only
- Patent, trademark, moral, publicity, and privacy rights are not licensed
- The license text includes disclaimer and limitation-of-liability language
- Downstream recipients automatically receive an offer to exercise the same licensed rights under the license terms
- Users may not impose additional restrictions or technical measures that would block exercise of the licensed rights
- The license preserves exceptions and limitations such as fair use where they apply

## Notes

- Creative Commons is not a party to its public licenses
- In the license text, the organization distinguishes between the license language and the public-domain dedication of the text itself
- The name is often used interchangeably with the license family, but the organization is distinct from any specific work licensed under [[concepts/attribution-based-reuse]]
- The licensing file for the `agent-ready-context` skill separates CC-BY-4.0 content from Apache-2.0 code, reinforcing the organization’s role in a mixed-license repository
- The license text also notes that the Creative Commons trademark is not authorized for use without prior written consent, except as permitted by policy
- The same CC BY 4.0 text appears across multiple repository license files, including the `subagent-profile-adapter` skill package
