# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project welcomes contributions and discussion from anyone, regardless
of background or experience level. Participants are expected to treat each
other with courtesy and good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before assuming bad faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions —
and to any other setting where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be sent to the maintainer via
[GitHub](https://github.com/rmonier). Reports will be reviewed and handled
at the maintainer's discretion, proportionate to the impact of the
behavior — from a private warning up to a permanent ban from project
spaces for repeated or severe violations. Reporters' privacy will be
respected throughout.
